## Platform

web

## Stack

Existing: static HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript deployed to GitHub Pages at guts.gutors.github.io. No build step, no framework, no package manager. New work stays on that stack unless the user asks otherwise; anything added must run from plain files served over HTTP.

## Users

Primary: recruiters and hiring managers evaluating Gustavo for a full-time software engineering role, usually Brazilian or remote-international, screening several candidates in one sitting and often on a phone. They arrive from LinkedIn or a CV link, spend well under a minute, and want to answer three questions fast: what has this person actually shipped, can they be trusted with a real system, and how do I reach them.

Secondary: engineers who click through from GitHub and judge the work, not the bio.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio that converts a skim into a conversation. Success is a recruiter who understands the professional track record within one screen and leaves with the contact path in hand, having seen evidence of engineering craft rather than a list of self-descriptions.

The rare combination is institutional-scale backend experience plus demonstrated frontend craft, and the site itself is the frontend proof.

## Product Principles

1. Show the system, not the adjective. Every claim about engineering ability is carried by something the visitor can inspect: a described system, a repository, or the page's own behaviour.
2. A recruiter's minute is the budget. The professional record and the contact path both land inside the first screen and stay reachable from anywhere on the page.
3. Confidentiality is a design constraint, not a disclaimer. Real work is described by shape and scale, never by proprietary detail.
4. The site is a work sample. Its craft is evidence, so performance, accessibility, and responsiveness are part of the portfolio, not chores around it.
5. Nothing invented. Synthetic interface content is labelled; claims without a source are cut.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No product-specific standard was established by the user. Baseline expectation for a hiring surface: WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, full keyboard operation including any interactive demonstration, visible focus, reduced-motion respect, and readable at 200% zoom on a phone.
