What I actually built

Twelve years of software, nine of them inside Porto Alegre's public treasury. Below is the work behind the figures above, written at the level of system and decision. Nothing here is confidential: no client data, no internal names.

2015 — 2024

Senior Software EngineerProcempa · Porto Alegre

Treasury sector: every system behind the city's public spending. I led the architecture of the pharmaceutical inventory used across 172 health locations, and rewrote how the city's services talk to each other.

  • Pharmaceutical inventory, 172 locations. Designed and led the city-wide system that tracks medicine stock across the free public pharmacy network. It saved more than US$ 800k (R$ 4M) in its first year by preventing expiration and theft — the figure at the top of this page.
  • pessoaAPI. Built the RESTful API that centralised citizen data for more than ten internal systems, cutting duplicated records and unblocking contracts with new business clients.
  • RabbitMQ between services. Replaced synchronous REST calls with message queues where the bottleneck was coupling, not throughput. Job execution got 80% faster.
  • Query and persistence work. Native SQL and fetch strategies in Hibernate took search latency down 70% for thousands of daily clinical users.
  • Fiscal reporting, automated. Replaced manual data entry for BNAFAR and EFD-REINF, returning hundreds of hours a month to more than a hundred colleagues and keeping the city at 100% fiscal compliance.
  • Monolith to microservices. Co-led the migration, with observability and an Angular frontend that replaced the legacy screens.

Java 8, 12, 21 · Spring Boot · WildFly · Hibernate · RabbitMQ · Angular · TypeScript · RxJS · PostgreSQL · Oracle · IBM DB2 · Keycloak · Docker · Jenkins
